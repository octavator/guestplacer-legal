Privacy Policy for Guestplacer

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Introduction

Guestplacer is a mobile app for iOS and Android that helps you seat guests at tables for weddings, parties, galas and team dinners. It has no user accounts, no login, no servers of our own, no analytics and no advertising. Your events and guest lists stay on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, how the app handles information. In short: everything you enter stays on your device. The only data that leaves it is a technical check for app updates, which carries a random installation ID and contains none of your content. The sections below give the detail.

This policy applies to the Guestplacer mobile app published by Théophile de Cagny (referred to as "we", "us", or "our").

What the App Does Not Do

Guestplacer does not:

  • Require you to create an account or sign in.
  • Collect your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information.
  • Access your location, camera, photo library, microphone, or contacts.
  • Use analytics, advertising, tracking, or social-media SDKs.
  • Assign you an advertising identifier or track you across apps or websites.
  • Send your events, guest lists, notes, or seating plans to us or to anyone else.

App Updates

Each time the app starts, it asks Expo's update service (EAS Update) whether a newer version of Guestplacer is available, and downloads it if so. This lets us fix bugs without waiting for a store release. The request carries only the technical information needed to deliver the right update:

  • An installation ID: a random identifier created on your device for this installation of the app. It is not linked to your name, your email, or your Apple or Google account, and a new one is created if you delete and reinstall the app.
  • App and platform details: iOS or Android, the app version, the update channel, and which update is installed.
  • Launch errors: if an update failed to start, the IDs of the failed updates and the short technical error message (up to 1,024 characters) from the failed launch, sent once.
  • Your IP address, which any internet request reveals.

Expo keeps the installation ID and IP address with its record of each request, so it can count how many installations have each update and roll updates out gradually. It deletes the error message after three days. Expo processes this data on our behalf as a service provider; it does not use it for advertising or share it with data brokers. See Expo's privacy policy.

The app never puts your events, guest names, notes, or seating plans in these requests. If your device is offline, the check simply fails and the app keeps working with the version you have.

This is what our store listings declare: on the Apple App Store, Identifiers (Device ID) and Diagnostics (Crash Data), not linked to you and not used for tracking; on Google Play, Device or other IDs and Crash logs, collected but not shared.

Data Stored on Your Device

Everything you create in Guestplacer is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. This includes:

  • Your events: title, date, and type (wedding, party, corporate, blank).
  • Your guest lists: names, tags, whether each guest is attending or is a child, and any notes you add (for example dietary needs).
  • Your tables, seating, and any seating rules you set.
  • Your preferences: language and light or dark theme.

This data is not synced to any cloud and not sent to us; we have no servers to receive it. If your device makes its own backups (for example iCloud Backup or Google backup), the app's data may be included in them. That is handled by your device and your backup settings, not by Guestplacer.

Your Guests' Information

A guest list is information about other people. You decide what to enter. Please add only what you need to plan the seating, and take care with sensitive details such as health or dietary notes. This information stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

Files You Share or Import

Guestplacer only sends your content somewhere when you ask it to. When you export a PDF (seating plan, caterer list, place cards, escort cards), a CSV file, a JSON backup, or send an event to your partner as a .guestplacer file, the app creates the file on your device and opens your system's share sheet or print dialog. You choose where it goes: AirDrop, Messages, WhatsApp, Mail, Files, a printer. From then on, the file is handled by the app or the person you sent it to, under their own terms. We never receive it.

When you import, the app reads only the file you pick in the system file picker, or the text on your clipboard when you tap Paste, and processes it on your device.

Third-Party Services

Guestplacer uses a deliberately small set of third-party services, none of them for advertising or tracking:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play — to distribute the app. They handle your download under their own privacy policies: Apple, Google.
  • Expo (EAS Update) — to deliver app updates, as described in "App Updates" above: https://expo.dev/privacy.

There are no analytics, advertising, tracking, or social-media SDKs in the app, and no crash reporting beyond the launch errors described above.

Permissions

Guestplacer does not request any device permissions. It does not ask for access to your camera, location, photos, contacts, or microphone.

The file picker, share sheet, and print dialog are run by your device's system, and give the app only what you choose. On iOS, the system may ask you to allow pasting the first time you tap Paste.

Children's Privacy

Guestplacer is a general-audience app for planning events. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The guest lists you create may include children's names; that information stays on your device.

Data Deletion

Because your content is stored on your device, you are always in control of it.

  • To delete an event, open its Settings tab and tap Delete event, or long-press it on the home screen.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device, including the installation ID. A reinstalled app gets a new one.
  • We hold no copy of your events, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Expo's update records are not linked to your identity, so we cannot look them up from your name or email; if you have a question about them, contact us.
  • Files you have exported or shared stay wherever you saved or sent them.

Your Rights

If you are in the European Union, the United Kingdom, or another place with data-protection laws, you have the right to access, correct, and delete personal data about you, and to object to its use. Because we hold no account, profile, or content about you, there is usually nothing for us to provide or delete. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and keep them only as long as needed to help you. You can also complain to your data-protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

International Users

Guestplacer can be used anywhere the app stores are available. Your events stay on your device, wherever you are. Update requests are handled by Expo, which may process them in the United States and in other countries where it or its providers operate, as described in its privacy policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes in the app or in legal requirements. When we make changes, we will update the "Effective date" at the top of this page and post the revised policy at the same location. Significant changes may also be noted within the app. Your continued use of Guestplacer after an update means you accept the revised policy.
