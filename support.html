Common questions

Do I need an internet connection?
No. Guestplacer works fully offline. It only goes online when it starts, to check for an app update.

How do I share the plan with my partner?
In the event, open Settings → Send to partner (or long-press the event on the home screen) and send the file by AirDrop, WhatsApp or Mail. On the other phone, open the file with Guestplacer, or tap Import a backup on the home screen. It can be added as a new event, merged into the guest list already there, or replace it.

I'm changing phones. How do I keep my events?
Your events are stored only on your phone, and deleting the app deletes them. Before you switch, open each event's Settings → Full backup (JSON) and save or send the file. On the new phone, tap Import a backup on the home screen. Moving to a new phone from an iCloud or Google backup usually brings your events along as well.

How do I import my guest list?
In the event, open Settings → Import guests. Paste rows copied from Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, or open a CSV file. Columns: Name, Tags (comma-separated), Attending, Child, Notes; English or French headers both work. Guests already in the event are matched by name and updated.

My place cards print at the wrong size.
Print the PDF at 100 % (“Actual size”) on A4 paper, not “Fit to page”.

Which languages does the app speak?
English and French. It follows your phone's language; change it in Settings → App → Language.
